# AI Builder Cup 2026 — brief and submission checklist

**Reviewed:** 18 October 2026  
**Official pages:** [Overview](https://aibuildercup.com/index.html) · [Themes & Requirements](https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html) · [FAQs](https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html) · [Rewards](https://aibuildercup.com/rewards.html)

## What the competition is about

The Google Cloud AI Builder Cup 2026, powered by Hack2skill, is a JAPAC-region hackathon. Teams choose a real problem, build a working AI prototype using Google Cloud, and submit it for evaluation. Shortlisted teams are invited to demo in person in Singapore.

The central idea is to build for a local or industry need and show how the solution could scale. A pitch deck or mockup alone is not enough: the prototype must work end to end.

## Choose one theme

1. **BFSI — Intelligent Risk, Fraud & Financial Experiences:** fraud/risk, complex process automation, customer interactions, and personalized financial experiences.
2. **Retail & Commerce — Intelligent Customer and Business Experiences:** discovery, personalization, conversational shopping, demand planning, inventory, customer insight, or fraud.
3. **Manufacturing — Intelligent Operations & Industrial Efficiency:** efficiency, quality, reliability, supply and demand, sustainability, and turning operational data into useful actions or insights.
4. **Media, Content & Digital Experiences:** creating, discovering, personalizing, understanding, or experiencing content across media, education, publishing, communication, and related fields.
5. **Future of Work & Enterprise Productivity:** knowledge access, collaboration, decisions, and everyday workflows.
6. **Sustainability & Social Impact:** environmental or societal challenges, including resource efficiency, accessibility, resilience, communities, and sustainable decisions.

Submit under one problem statement/theme only.

## Eligibility and team rules

- Participants must be **21 or older**, based in the **JAPAC region**, and be working professionals, entrepreneurs, or startup builders.
- **Students are not eligible.** A team that includes a student can be disqualified, including after shortlisting.
- Teams must have **2–4 members** during the build. You may register solo and form or join a team later, but you cannot compete solo.
- Cross-country teams are allowed if every member is based in JAPAC.
- A participant cannot join multiple teams or submit multiple solutions.
- If selected for the Singapore finale, only **two members** per team may attend, regardless of team size.

## What you must build

- A functional, end-to-end prototype that addresses the chosen problem.
- Use Google AI models such as **Gemini or Gemma**, or an eligible Google agentic platform such as **Agent Platform, Antigravity, or AI Studio**.
- Deploy the prototype on Google Cloud using **Cloud Run or Firebase**. The FAQ says solutions built primarily on other cloud platforms are not eligible.
- The project must be newly built during the hackathon timeline; previously existing projects or work started before program launch are not eligible.
- Design for usability, accessibility, and plausible scalability.
- Submission materials, code, documentation, and presentation must be in English.

## What to submit

The official FAQ lists these deliverables:

- **Live deployed prototype link** (working deployment on the required Google Cloud platform).
- **Public GitHub repository** for the prototype.
- **Video demo under 3 minutes** showing the AI solution in action; the themes page specifies a public YouTube/Vimeo video or public Google Drive link.
- **Solution deck** explaining the idea and implementation. The themes page says submit a deck/PPT converted to PDF.
- **Clear problem/theme identification** and explanation of the problem, intended beneficiaries, and positive impact.
- Evidence of **feasibility and scalability**; ensure the prototype demonstrates the core capability rather than only describing it.

### Practical upload checklist

- [ ] Pick one of the six themes and state the exact problem being addressed.
- [ ] Confirm every teammate meets the age, location, and non-student eligibility rules.
- [ ] Build a working prototype using the required Google AI/agent tooling.
- [ ] Deploy it on Cloud Run or Firebase and check the link is accessible to reviewers.
- [ ] Publish the source code in a public GitHub repository.
- [ ] Record a public video demo that is less than 3 minutes.
- [ ] Create the solution deck and export it as a PDF.
- [ ] Write all submission materials in English.
- [ ] Ensure the prototype, repository, video, and PDF links are public and work without special access.

## How entries are judged

| Criterion | Weight | What judges look for |
|---|---:|---|
| Technical Merit & Gen AI Implementation | 40% | Meaningful use of GenAI, technical soundness, working implementation, and scalability |
| Problem Alignment & Impact | 25% | Fit to the selected problem and clear value to users or stakeholders |
| Innovation & Creativity | 25% | Originality, differentiation, and a thoughtful use of GenAI |
| User Experience & Solution Design | 10% | Usability, accessibility, and a coherent experience |
